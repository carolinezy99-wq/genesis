Reading and trend

This is an analytical reading of the figures on this page. It is not official central-bank guidance, and it is not a forecast. Each bank's one-line reading is on that bank's page.

  1. Of the latest decisions, 5 are tightening, 4 are holding, and 0 are easing.
  2. Inflation is above the target used on this page in US, Euro area, UK, Canada, India, Australia. It is below target in Japan, China. MAS has no point target.
  3. US, Euro area, Australia tightened at the latest decision while inflation was still above target. UK, Canada, India are holding with inflation still above target.
  4. The highest policy rate is India at 5.25%. The lowest among rate-setting banks is Japan at around 1.25%.

Data as of 2026-10-03. Figures were read from central-bank and official statistics pages on that date; the site does not re-fetch them.

Germany, France and Italy are covered by the ECB. The inflation gap is computed on this page from the figures shown; it is not official central-bank language.
